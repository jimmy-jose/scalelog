# Code of conduct

ScaleLog welcomes people of every background, identity, ability, and level of experience. Everyone participating in this project deserves a respectful environment in which to learn, contribute, and disagree.

## Participating respectfully

- Treat questions and contributions with patience, especially when someone is new to system design, React Native, or open source.
- Discuss the work and its tradeoffs. Explain technical disagreements without attacking the person making a proposal.
- Give specific, constructive feedback, accept corrections, and take responsibility when your behavior causes harm.
- Respect other people's time, boundaries, and privacy.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks, and deliberate intimidation are unacceptable. Do not publish someone's personal information without their permission. Repeatedly disrupting discussions, targeting contributors across platforms, or continuing unwanted contact is also unacceptable.

## Scope

These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project community spaces. They also apply when someone publicly represents ScaleLog in an official capacity.

## Reporting concerns

Email **Jimmy Jose** at [jimmy.jose96@gmail.com](mailto:jimmy.jose96@gmail.com) to report a concern privately. Include links to relevant interactions, what happened, and any immediate concerns about further contact. You do not need to confront the person involved or report the incident publicly.

Reports will be reviewed with care. Information will be shared only as needed to investigate and respond; the reporter's identity will be protected wherever possible. If a concern involves the maintainer or you prefer an independent reporting route for conduct on GitHub, use [GitHub's abuse reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove inappropriate content and restrict participation. Responses depend on the severity and pattern of the conduct: a request to correct behavior, a warning, a temporary restriction, or a permanent ban. Serious threats or harassment may result in immediate removal.

Anyone affected by an enforcement decision can email the reporting address to ask for clarification or reconsideration. Maintainers and frequent contributors are held to the same standards as everyone else.
